# Harbourline Health — Gold PPO Plan (United States)

Summary of benefits and coverage, version 2026.1. Effective January 1, 2026.

*Made-up policy for a learning project. Harbourline Health is not a real insurer.*

## 1. Definitions

**Deductible:** the amount you pay each plan year for covered services before the plan starts to pay.

**Coinsurance:** your share of the cost of a covered service, as a percentage, after you meet your deductible.

**Out-of-pocket maximum:** the most you pay in a plan year for covered services. After you reach it, the plan pays 100%.

**In-network provider:** a doctor or hospital that has a contract with Harbourline Health.

**Prior authorization:** approval from us before you receive a service, confirming that it is medically necessary.

**Plan year:** January 1 to December 31.

## 2. Cost sharing

The deductible is $1,500 per individual and $3,000 per family for in-network care, and $3,000 per individual and $6,000 per family for out-of-network care. After the deductible, you pay 20% coinsurance for in-network care and 40% coinsurance for out-of-network care. The out-of-pocket maximum is $6,000 per individual and $12,000 per family for in-network care, and $12,000 per individual and $24,000 per family for out-of-network care. Amounts you pay for out-of-network care do not count toward the in-network out-of-pocket maximum.

## 3. Hospital and surgery

Inpatient hospitalization, including surgery, surgeon and anesthesia fees, is covered after the deductible at the coinsurance rates in section 2.

## 4. Emergency care

Emergency room visits have a $350 copay per visit, which is waived if you are admitted to the hospital. Emergency services from out-of-network providers are covered at the in-network cost-sharing level.

## 5. Prior authorization

Prior authorization is required for all non-emergency inpatient admissions, outpatient surgery, and advanced imaging (MRI, CT and PET scans). If you do not get prior authorization, we reduce benefits for that service by 50%, up to a maximum reduction of $2,500.

For an emergency inpatient admission, you or the hospital must notify us within 2 business days after admission.

## 6. Coverage outside the United States

Care received outside the United States is covered only for emergencies. We reimburse emergency care abroad at the in-network cost-sharing level, up to the usual and customary charge for the same service in the United States. You must pay the provider and then submit a claim with itemized bills translated into English. Planned or non-emergency care outside the United States is not covered.

## 7. Waiting periods

There are no waiting periods under this plan.

## 8. Exclusions

We do not cover:

1. Cosmetic surgery.
2. Experimental or investigational treatment.
3. Weight-loss surgery, unless you meet our medical criteria and receive prior authorization.
4. Non-emergency care received outside the United States.
5. Long-term custodial care.
