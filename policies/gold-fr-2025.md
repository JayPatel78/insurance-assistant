# Harbourline Health — Gold Health Plan (France)

Policy wording, version 2025.1. In force from 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025. Replaced by version 2026.1.

*Made-up policy for a learning project. Harbourline Health is not a real insurer.*

## 1. Definitions

**You / the member:** the person named on the Certificate of Insurance.

**Home country:** France, including its overseas departments.

**Territorial area:** the member states of the European Union and the European Economic Area (EEA).

**Emergency:** a sudden illness or injury that needs treatment within 24 hours to avoid serious harm to your health.

**Pre-authorisation:** our written agreement, given before treatment starts, that we will pay for it.

## 2. Hospital benefits in France

We pay 100% of the cost of in-patient hospital treatment in France, including surgery, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, intensive care and nursing care. A private room is covered up to €120 per night.

The overall annual limit for all benefits under this plan is €750,000 per member per policy year.

## 3. Treatment abroad

### 3.1 Emergency treatment in the territorial area

If you have an emergency while temporarily travelling in the territorial area outside France, we pay for emergency treatment, including emergency surgery, up to €100,000 per policy year. An excess of €250 applies to each claim for treatment abroad.

Cover applies to trips of up to 60 consecutive days.

### 3.2 Emergency treatment outside the territorial area

Emergency treatment outside the territorial area is covered up to €25,000 per policy year, excluding the United States of America and Canada.

### 3.3 Planned treatment abroad

Planned treatment abroad is not covered.

## 4. Pre-authorisation

All planned in-patient admissions and all planned surgery need pre-authorisation, requested at least 10 working days before the treatment date.

Emergency admissions do not need pre-authorisation in advance, but Harbourline Assistance must be notified within 24 hours of the emergency admission. If we are not notified within 24 hours, we reduce the amount we pay for that admission by 30%.

## 5. Out-patient benefits

- GP and specialist consultations: 100% of the French social security reference rate, plus up to 150% for practitioners who charge additional fees.
- Physiotherapy: up to 20 sessions per policy year.

## 6. Waiting periods

There is no waiting period for accidents or emergencies. Planned surgery has a waiting period of 6 months from the start of your membership. Maternity has a waiting period of 12 months.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not pay for cosmetic surgery, treatment received in the United States of America or Canada, experimental treatment, or treatment abroad if you travelled for the purpose of obtaining treatment.

## 8. Important information

This document describes your cover. It is not a guarantee of payment.
