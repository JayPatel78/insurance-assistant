# Harbourline Health — Gold Health Plan (France)

Policy wording, version 2026.1. In force from 1 January 2026.

*Made-up policy for a learning project. Harbourline Health is not a real insurer.*

## 1. Definitions

**You / the member:** the person named on the Certificate of Insurance.

**Home country:** France, including its overseas departments.

**Territorial area:** the member states of the European Union, the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland and the United Kingdom.

**Emergency:** a sudden illness or injury that needs treatment within 24 hours to avoid serious harm to your health, such as acute appendicitis, a heart attack or a fracture.

**Planned treatment:** any treatment that is not an emergency.

**Pre-authorisation:** our written agreement, given before treatment starts, that we will pay for it.

**Policy year:** the 12 months from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Hospital benefits in France

We pay 100% of the cost of in-patient hospital treatment in France, including surgery, surgeon and anaesthetist fees, intensive care, a private room and nursing care.

Day-case surgery (admitted and discharged on the same day) is paid at 100%.

The overall annual limit for all benefits under this plan is €1,000,000 per member per policy year.

## 3. Treatment abroad

### 3.1 Emergency treatment in the territorial area

If you have an emergency while temporarily travelling in the territorial area outside France, we pay for emergency in-patient and out-patient treatment, including emergency surgery, up to €150,000 per policy year. An excess of €150 applies to each claim for treatment abroad.

Cover applies to trips of up to 90 consecutive days. Treatment needed on day 91 or later of a trip is not covered.

### 3.2 Emergency treatment outside the territorial area

Emergency treatment in the rest of the world, excluding the United States of America, is covered up to €50,000 per policy year, with the same €150 excess per claim.

### 3.3 Planned treatment abroad

Planned treatment abroad is covered only with pre-authorisation, and is paid at the rates that would apply to the same treatment in France.

### 3.4 Medical repatriation

If our medical team decides it is medically necessary, we organise and pay for your transport back to a hospital in France, without limit.

## 4. Pre-authorisation

All planned in-patient admissions and all planned surgery need pre-authorisation. Ask for it at least 5 working days before the treatment date by calling the Harbourline Assistance line.

Emergency admissions do not need pre-authorisation in advance. However, you, a relative or the hospital must notify Harbourline Assistance within 48 hours of the emergency admission. If we are not notified within 48 hours, we reduce the amount we pay for that admission by 20%.

## 5. Out-patient benefits

- GP and specialist consultations: 100% of the French social security reference rate (tarif de convention), plus up to 200% for practitioners who charge additional fees.
- Physiotherapy: up to 25 sessions per policy year.
- Prescription medicines: 100% of the cost of medicines reimbursed by French social security.

## 6. Maternity

Pregnancy and childbirth are covered up to €6,000 per pregnancy. A waiting period of 10 months applies from the start of your membership.

## 7. Waiting periods

There is no waiting period for accidents or emergencies. Planned surgery has a waiting period of 3 months from the start of your membership. Maternity has a waiting period of 10 months (see section 6).

## 8. Exclusions

We do not pay for:

1. Cosmetic surgery, unless it is needed to repair damage caused by an accident covered by this plan.
2. Any treatment received in the United States of America.
3. Treatment abroad if you travelled against the advice of a doctor, or travelled for the purpose of obtaining treatment, unless that treatment was pre-authorised.
4. Experimental or unproven treatment.
5. Injuries resulting from taking part in professional sport or in motor racing.
6. Treatment needed because of the abuse of alcohol or drugs.

## 9. Making a claim

Send your claim within 6 months of the date of treatment, with itemised invoices and, for treatment abroad, proof of your travel dates. Claims for treatment abroad are paid in euros at the exchange rate on the date of treatment.

## 10. Important information

This document describes your cover. It is not a guarantee of payment for any particular treatment. Contact Harbourline Assistance to confirm cover before planned treatment.
