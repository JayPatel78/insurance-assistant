# Harbourline Health — Essential Health Plan (France)

Policy wording, version 2026.1. In force from 1 January 2026.

*Made-up policy for a learning project. Harbourline Health is not a real insurer.*

## 1. Definitions

**You / the member:** the person named on the Certificate of Insurance.

**Certificate of Insurance:** the document we send you that lists your plan and any add-ons you have bought.

**Emergency:** a sudden illness or injury that needs treatment within 24 hours to avoid serious harm to your health.

**Pre-authorisation:** our written agreement, given before treatment starts, that we will pay for it.

## 2. Hospital benefits

Emergency hospitalisation, including emergency surgery, is covered in full.

Planned in-patient treatment is covered at 100% of the French social security reference rate. Costs above the reference rate are not covered. A private room is not covered.

The overall annual limit for all benefits under this plan is €100,000 per member per policy year.

## 3. Where you are covered

This plan covers treatment received in metropolitan France only. Treatment received in any other country is not covered, including emergency treatment, unless you have the Essential Travel add-on.

### 3.1 Essential Travel add-on

If the Essential Travel add-on is shown on your Certificate of Insurance, emergency treatment received in the European Union or the European Economic Area is covered up to €10,000 per trip, for trips of up to 30 consecutive days. An excess of €100 applies to each claim made under the add-on. Planned treatment abroad is never covered.

## 4. Pre-authorisation

All planned in-patient admissions need pre-authorisation. Emergency admissions do not need pre-authorisation in advance, but Harbourline Assistance must be notified within 48 hours.

## 5. Out-patient benefits

- GP consultations: 100% of the French social security reference rate.
- Specialist consultations: 100% of the reference rate, only with a referral from your GP.
- Physiotherapy: not covered.

## 6. Waiting periods

There is no waiting period for accidents or emergencies. Planned in-patient treatment has a waiting period of 6 months.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not pay for:

1. Cosmetic surgery.
2. Treatment outside metropolitan France, except as described in section 3.1.
3. Dental and optical treatment.
4. Maternity, pregnancy and childbirth.
5. Experimental or unproven treatment.

## 8. Important information

This document describes your cover. It is not a guarantee of payment for any particular treatment.
