# Harbourline Health — Silver Health Plan (France)

Policy wording, version 2026.1. In force from 1 January 2026.

*Made-up policy for a learning project. Harbourline Health is not a real insurer.*

## 1. Definitions

**You / the member:** the person named on the Certificate of Insurance.

**Home country:** France.

**Territorial area:** the member states of the European Union and the European Economic Area (EEA). Switzerland and the United Kingdom are not part of the territorial area.

**Emergency:** a sudden illness or injury that needs treatment within 24 hours to avoid serious harm to your health.

**Accident:** a sudden, unexpected external event that causes bodily injury. An illness, including a sudden one such as appendicitis, is not an accident.

**Pre-authorisation:** our written agreement, given before treatment starts, that we will pay for it.

## 2. Hospital benefits in France

We pay 80% of the cost of in-patient hospital treatment in France, including surgery and surgeon fees. You pay the remaining 20% (your co-payment). A private room is not covered.

The overall annual limit for all benefits under this plan is €250,000 per member per policy year.

## 3. Treatment abroad

### 3.1 Emergency treatment in the territorial area

If you have an emergency while temporarily travelling in the territorial area outside France, we pay for emergency treatment up to €30,000 per policy year. Each surgical procedure performed abroad is limited to €15,000, including surgeon, anaesthetist and theatre fees. An excess of €300 applies to each claim for treatment abroad.

Cover applies to trips of up to 30 consecutive days.

### 3.2 Treatment outside the territorial area

There is no cover for any treatment outside the territorial area, including emergencies.

### 3.3 Planned treatment abroad

Planned treatment abroad is not covered.

## 4. Pre-authorisation

All planned in-patient admissions and all planned surgery need pre-authorisation, requested at least 10 working days before the treatment date.

Emergency admissions do not need pre-authorisation in advance. Harbourline Assistance must be notified within 24 hours of an emergency admission. If we are not notified within 24 hours, we may refuse the claim for that admission.

## 5. Out-patient benefits

- GP and specialist consultations: 70% of the French social security reference rate.
- Physiotherapy: up to 10 sessions per policy year.

## 6. Waiting periods

A waiting period of 30 days from the start of your membership applies to all treatment, including emergency treatment. The only exception is treatment needed because of an accident, which has no waiting period.

Planned surgery has a waiting period of 6 months. Maternity is not covered under this plan.

## 7. Exclusions

We do not pay for:

1. Cosmetic surgery.
2. Any treatment outside the territorial area.
3. Maternity, pregnancy and childbirth.
4. Experimental or unproven treatment.
5. Treatment abroad if you travelled for the purpose of obtaining treatment.

## 8. Important information

This document describes your cover. It is not a guarantee of payment for any particular treatment.
